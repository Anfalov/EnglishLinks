## EnglishLinks 0.5.0

- Fixed English profession recipe links: labels now contain the recipe name
  without the profession prefix, preserving the recipe ID, link type and color.
  The fix was confirmed in-game on WoW Forever.
- Added the EnglishLinks icon and an in-game project screenshot.
- Includes bundled English names for 24,020 items, 31,703 spells and 4,276 quests,
  plus skills, achievements, currencies, companions and map names.
- No client-side name collection and no required addon dependencies.

Target: WoW Forever 1.60.1, Interface 16001.

Known limitations: name coverage is incomplete; unknown names and random-affix
items retain their original labels. Some companion links fail to send even
without translation. Retail and other Classic editions are not validated.
