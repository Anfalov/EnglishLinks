EnglishLinks

Share game links in English while playing WoW Forever in your own language.

EnglishLinks automatically changes the names of links you insert into chat to English. Other players receive English link labels, while your interface stays in your client language. They do not need the addon installed.

It is built for WoW Forever.

English links in chat. Translated links are clickable.

Supported links

  • Items, including suffixes such as of the Bear and of the Owl.
  • Spells and abilities.
  • Quests.
  • Professions and recipes.
  • Achievements, currencies, mounts, companion pets and map pins.

Links remain clickable. As with any WoW link, each player sees the tooltip in their own client's language.

How to use

Install and enable EnglishLinks, then open chat and Shift-click a link as usual. Its name changes to English in the chat input, ready to share.

For manual installation, place the EnglishLinks folder in your WoW Forever client's Interface/AddOns folder.

Main commands

  • /el on / /el off — turn English link names on or off.
  • /el — show all available commands.
